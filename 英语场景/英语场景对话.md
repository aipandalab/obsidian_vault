## 1. Meeting on the street
> A: Excuse me. Do I know you?
> B: Maybe. You look familiar too.
> A: Did you go to Central High School?
> B: Yes, I did. Ten years ago.
> A: Then I know you. We were classmate.
> B: That's right. I remember you now.
> A: It has been a long time.
> B: Yes. Many years have passed.
> A: How are you these days?
> B: I'm well, thank you. And you?
> A: I'm doing well too.
> B: Do you still live in this city?
> A: Yes. I never moved away.
> B: I moved back last year.
> A: Really? I didn't know that.
> B: Yes. I worked in another city before.
> A: What do you do now?
> B: I work in a bank downtown.
> A: That sounds good.
> B: And what about you?
> A: I work in a school office.
> B: That suits you very well.
> A: Thank you. I like there.
> B: Are you in a hurry now.
> A: Not really. I'm just walking home.
> B: I'm going to a bakery.
> A: The bakery on Maple Street?
> B: Yes. That one. It's very good.
> A: I know it. Their bread is excellent.
> B: Exactly. I go there very Saturday.
> A: Do you still play football?
> B: No, not anymore. Just sometimes with friends.
> A: I remember you played very weekend.
> B: That was a long time ago.
> A: Yes, but I remember it well.
> B: Do you keep in touch with anyone from school?
> A: Only one friend. Not many.
> B: Same for me.
> A: It is funny to meet here.
> B: Yes. The street is full of surprises.
> A: We should have coffee sometime.
> B: Yes. I would like that.
> A: Let's exchange numbers.
> B: Good idea. Here is my phone.
> A: Thank you. I will save your number.
> B: And I will save yours.
> A: Perfect. It was nice to see you again.
> B: It was nice to see you too.
> A: Have a good afternoon.
> B: You too. Goodbye.
> A: Goodbye.

## 2. Order a coffee
> A: Good morning.
> B: Good morning. What would you like?
> A: I would like a coffee, please.
> B: Of course. What kind of coffee?
> A: What do you have?
> B: We have espresso, latte, cappuccino and black coffee.
> A: I will have a cappuccino, please.
> B: Small, medium, or larger?
> A: Medium, please.
> B: Would you like regular milk or oat milk?
> A: Regular milk is fine.
> B: Of course. Anything to eat?
> A: Yes. What do you recommend?
> B: The croissants are very fresh today.
> A: Plain or chocolate? (原味还是巧克力)
> B: We have both.
> A: I will have one plain croissant.
> B: Good choice. It is warm right now.
> A: That sounds perfect.
> B: Would you like butter or jam (黄油还是果酱)
> A: A little butter, please. 
> B: Of course. Anything else? (还要别的吗？)
> A: No, thank you. That is all.
> B: Will you stay here or take it away?
> A: I will stay here
> B: Good. You can sit by the window.
> A: Thank you. It is quiet there.
> B: Yes. It is a nice table.
> A: How much is everything?
> B: The cappuccino is four dollars.
> A: And the croissant?
> B: Two dollars fifty cents.
> A: So six dollars fifty cents total.
> B: Yes. Exactly.
> A: Here is my card.
> B: Please tap here.
> A: Done.
> B: Payment accepted. Thank you.
> A: Thank you. How long will it take?
> B: only a few minutes.
> A: Good. I have some time.
> B: Are you going to work after this?
> A: Yes. My office is nearby.
> B: That is convenient. (那很方便)
> A: Yes. I come here sometimes in the morning.
> B: That is nice. We open at seven every day.
> A: Good to know. I like quiet morning.
> B: Then morning is the best time.
> A: I agree.
> B: Here is your cappuccino.
> A: Thank you very much
> B: And your croissant is coming now.
> A: Perfect. Thank you for your help.
> B: You are welcome. Enjoy your breakfast.
> A: Thank you. Goodbye for now. （暂别了）
> B: Goodbye.

## 3. Hotel check-in
> A: Good afternoon. I have a reservation.
> B: Good afternoon. Welcome. What name is the reservation under?
> A: It is under Laura Bennett.
> B: Laura Bennett. Let me check.
> A: Of course.
> B: Yes, I found it. One room for two nights.
> A: Yes, that is correct.
> B: May I see your passport, please?
> A:  Yes. Here it is.
> B: Thank you very much.
> A: You are welcome. (不客气)
> B: Would you like one key or two?
> A: Just one key, please. I am traveling alone.
> B: Of course. Your room is on the third floor.
> A: What number is the room?
> B: Room 305
> A: Is there an elevator?
> B: Yes. It is just behind you.
> A: Good. My suitcase is very heavy.
> B: If you need help, we can assist you.
> A: Thank you. I think I can manage.
> B: Breakfast is included with your stay.（住宿费包含早餐）
> A: Oh, that is good. What time is breakfast?
> B: From seven to ten in the morning.
> A: And where is it served?
> B: In the dining room on the ground floor.
> A: Perfect. Is there Wi-Fi in the room?
> B: Yes. Free Wi-Fi in all rooms.
> A: Wonderful. What is the password?
> B: It is written on this card.
> A: Thank you very much.
> B: Check-out is at eleven o'clock.
> A: Eleven. That is fine.
> B: Do you need anything else?
> A: Is there a restaurant nearby?
> B: Yes. There is a good one around the corner.
> A: That is helpful. I'm hungry.
> B: It serves dinner until ten thirty.
> A: Excellent. I still have time.
> B: Yes, plenty of time.
> A: This hotel looks very nice.
> B: Thank you. We are happy you are here.
> A: Thank you for your help.
> B: You are very welcome.
> A: I will go to my room now.
> B: Of course. Enjoy your stay.
> A: Thank you. Have a good evening.
> B: You too. Goodbye.
> A: Goodby.

## 4. Buy Water
>A: Excuse me. Do you sell water here?
> B: Yes, of course. We have several kinds.
> A: I would like a bottle of water, please.
> B: Still water or sparkling water? (静水或苏打水)
> A: Still water, please.
> B: Small or large bottle.
> A: Large, please. I'm very thirsty.
> B: Of course. Here you are.
> A: Thank you. Is it cold?
> B: Yes, It came from the fridge.
> A: That is perfect. It is very hot today.
> B: Yes. Many people are buying drinks today.
> A: I can understand that.
> B: Would you like anything else?
> A: Do you have juice too?
> B: Yes, Orange, apple, and mango.
> A: Not today, thank you. Just the water.
> B: No problem.
> A: How much is the large bottle?
> B: It is two dollars.
> A: That is fine. Here is my card.
> B: Please tap here.
> A: Done
> B: Payment accepted. Thank you.
> A: Thank you. Can I open it here?
> B: Yes, of course.
> A: Good. I need a drink right now.
> B: The heat is strong this afternoon.
> A: Yes. I was walking for a long time.
> B: That explains it.
> A: Is there a bench nearby?
> B: Yes. Just outside, near the tree.
> A: That sounds nice.
> B: It is in the shade too.
> A: Even better.
> B: Then you can rest a little.
> A: Yes. I think I need that.
> B: Do you know this area well?
> A: No. I'm visiting for the weekend.
> B: Then welcome. It is a nice neighborhood.
> A: Thank you. Everyone has been very kind.
> B: That is good to hear.
> A: I am glad I stopped here.
> B: We are glad too.
> A: Thank you for your help
> B: You are welcome. Enjoy your water.
> A: Thank you. Have a good day.
> B: You too. Goodbye.
> A: Goodbye.
