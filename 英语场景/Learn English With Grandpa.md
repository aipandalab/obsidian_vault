A: Hello, where are you right now?
B: I am in kitchen now.
A: Something terrible happened on road today.
B: Oh no, what happened there exactly?
A: One fast car crossed beside me.
B: Did car hit you there?
A: No, but dirty water splashed everywhere. - **splash**：动词，意为“（液体）溅起、泼溅”。在这里用的是过去式 _splashed_（因为事情刚刚发生）。**everywhere**：副词，意为“到处、处处”。整个短语生动地描绘了液体四处飞溅的混乱场景。
B: Oh no, are your clothes dirty?
A: Yes, my office suit looks terrible.
B: That is really unlucky today.
A: I can't go office like this.
B: Where are you standing right now?
A: I'm outside office building now.
B: Can you clean clothes little there?
A: No, mud stains look very bad. - **mud**：名词，意为“泥、泥土”。- **stain**：名词，意为“污渍、污点”**。_mud stains_ 合起来就是指**“泥渍 / 泥巴留下的痕迹”。
