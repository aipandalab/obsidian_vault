> B: Perfect, thanks. Do you have a straw by any chance? 请问你手边有吸管吗？**by any chance**：非常地道且委婉的口语短语，通常放在句末，意思是“难道/刚好/万一/凑巧”，用来让询问显得更加客气礼貌，避免给对方带来强迫感。

> B: That's great. Good for the environment.
> A: Are you here every day selling drinks?
> B: Yes, except when it rains. Then nobody comes to the beach anyway. - **except when...**：连词搭配，意思是“除非在……的时候 / 除了……之外”。**Then**：在这里作副词，表示“在那种情况下 / 到了那时”。**nobody**：代词，意思是“没有人”。 **anyway**：副词，在句末表示“反正 / 无论如何”，用来语气转折，说明下雨天没人来海滩是顺理成章的事。
> A: Make sense. Do you get many customers on hot days like this. 有道理。像今天这么热的天，你们这儿顾客多吗？
> B: Oh yes, mostly between three and five p.m. That's rush hour for drinks. 那是饮料销售的高峰期。
> A: I'm not surprised at all. It's so hot. Everyone needs hydration. 我一点也不意外。天这么热，每个人都需要补充水分。
> B: Yes, people always want a drink or some ice cream when it's like this. 是的，这种天气大家总是想喝杯饮料或者吃点冰淇淋。
> A: Oh, you have ice cream too? I didn't notice!
> B: Yes, but it goes fast. By afternoon it's usually sold out. 是的，不过卖得很快。到下午通常就卖光了
> A: I'll definitely come back later then. What flavors? **What flavors?**：非常地道简练的口语提问，省略了前面的动词（即 _What flavors do you have?_），意思是“（有什么）口味/风味？
> B: Chocolate, vanilla, strawberry. The basics but good quality.  巧克力、香草和草莓味。虽然都是最基础的口味，但品质都很好。
> A: Sounds delicious. Here's three dollars for the juice.
> B: Thank you. And here's your fifty cents change.
> A: Keep it as a tip. Do you come to this beach a lot yourself. 不用找了，当小费吧。你自己平时也常来这个海滩吗？
> B: Every day for work! But before that, not really. 为了工作每天都来！但在摆摊之前，倒不大常来。
> A: No, I mean as a customer. It's my first time. I'm on vacation. 不是啦，我是指作为顾客（来玩）。我是第一次来，正在这儿度假呢。
> B: Oh, welcome then! Where are you visiting from? 哦，那非常欢迎你！你是从哪里过来玩的呀？
> A: From New York. Thanks. It's very pretty here. Much nicer than city beaches.
> B: Yes, and the water is clean today. Perfect for swimming.
> A: I'll definitely swim after my drink. Need to cool off. 我喝完饮料绝对要去游泳，得好好降降温/凉快一下。
> B: Watch out for the sun though. It's stronger than It seems.不过要注意防晒/当心晒伤哦，这里的太阳比看着要毒。**Watch out for...**：非常高频的提醒口语，意思是“注意…… / 当心……”。**though**：放在句尾或句中，作副词，表示“不过 / 但是”，用来给对方提个醒。
> A: Yes, I put on sunscreen. SPF 50. Learned that lesson before. 涂啦，我擦了防晒霜，SPF 50 的。以前吃过这方面的亏/受过教训。
> B: Good. Smart thinking. Enjoy the sun and your juice.
> A: Thanks a lot. Maybe see you later for that ice cream.
> B: You're welcome. I'll save some chocolate for you! See you!
