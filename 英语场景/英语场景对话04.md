> B: That is for delicate clothes. **delicate**：形容词，意为“脆弱的、娇贵的、需要精心护理的”（在洗衣语境中常指丝绸、羊毛等不能用力机洗的衣物）
> A: I just have shirts and jeans.
> B: Then normal wash is perfect.
> A: How long does it take?
> B: About forty minutes.
> A: That is not too long.
> B: No. Then you can use the dryer after. _dryer_ 是自助洗衣房里的烘干机
> A: Do I need separate coins? **separate coins**：名词短语，意为“单独的硬币 / 零钱”。
> B: Yes. Three dollars for the washer. Two for the dryer.
> A: Five dollars total. Okay.
> B: You can also hang clothes in your apartment. **hang clothes**：动词短语，意为“挂衣服、晾衣服”
> A: Yes, but I need my jeans today.
> B: Then the dryer is better.
> A: Thank you for explaining everything.
> B: Of course. We all learn this at first. - **Of course**：意为“当然 / 没问题”（用于爽快地答应对方的请求，或者表示事情本该如此）。**We all learn this**：意为“我们大家都会经历这个 / 都要学会这个”。 **at first**：意为“起初 / 刚开始的时候”。
> A: That is true. I felt a little lost. 确实如此。我刚才感到有点迷茫 / 不知所措。
> B: You are not lost now.
> A: No. Now I know what to do.
> B: Good. Press this green button to start.
> A: Like this?
> B: Yes. Perfect . It is running now.
> A: Wonderful. Thank you again.
> B: My pleasure. See you around the building. 别客气 / 不用谢。回头在这栋楼里见
> A: Yes. Have a good day.
> B: You too. Goodbye.
> A: Goodbye.

## 12. Shopping for clothes
> A: Excuse me. Can you help me, please?
> B: Of course. What are you looking for?
> A: I need a new shirt for work.
> B: For work. Do you want something formal? - _something_ 是复合不定代词，当有形容词修饰时，形容词需要后置（即 _formal something_ 是错误的，必须说 _something formal_）。_formal_ 意为“正式的、礼节性的”（对应工作职场中需要穿的商务正装或得体服装）。
> A: Yes. Simple and neat. **neat**：形容词，意为“整洁的、干净的、利落的”
> B: We have several good options here.
> A: I like this blue one.
> B: That blue shirt is very popular.
> A: It feels soft. What material is it?
> B: Cotton. One hundred percent cotton.
> A: That is good. It looks comfortable.
> B: Yes. And easy to wash.
> A: Do you have it in medium?
> B: Let me check. Yes. medium is here.
> A: Can I try it on?
> B: Of course. The fitting room is over there.
> A: Thank you. I will try it now.
> B: Take your time.
> A: I am back. What do you think?
> B: It looks very nice on you.
> A: Do you think it is too big?
> B: No. The size looks right.
> A: I like the color too.
> B: Blue is good for work. Very professional.
> A: Yes. That is what I want.
> B: Would you like to see another color too?
> A: Maybe white. White is always useful. 也许选白色吧。白色总是很百搭（或：永远派得上用场）。
> B: Yes. White goes with everything. _go with_ 在这里意为“搭配、协调”；
> A: Do you have the same shirt in white?
> B: Yes. Here it is.
> A: This one looks nice too.
> B: Many people buy both colors.
> A: That is a good idea. How much are they?
> B: The blue one is twenty-eight dollars. 
> A: And the white one?
> B: Also twenty-eight dollars.
> A: If I buy two, is there a discount?
> B: Yes. Two shirts are fifty dollars total.
> A: That is a good deal.
> B: Yes. You save six dollars.
> A: Then I will take both.
> B: Excellent choice.
> A: Can I pay by card?
> B: Yes, of course.
> A: Do you also sell ties?
> B: Yes. Just behind you, on that wall.
> A: Maybe next time. Today, only the shirts.
> B: No problem. I will ring these up now. **ring these up**：非常地道的商业零售短语，意为“（收银员）扫描条形码、为这些商品计价结账”。其中 _ring up_ 原本指老式收银机按键时发出的铃声，现在泛指收银员输入或扫描并计算商品总价。_these_ 指代画面中顾客选购的衣物。
> A: Thank you very much.
> B: You are welcome. Here is your receipt.
> A: Thank you. Have a nice day.
> B: You too. Goodbye.
> Goodbye.
