## 1. Visiting the Doctor
> A: Good morning. Please come in and sit down.
> B: Good morning, doctor. Thank you.
> A: What brings you in today? - **What brings you...**：非常地道的固定问候句式，意为“是什么风把你吹来的 / 是什么原因让你来到这里”。**bring... in**：动词短语，意为“把……引入 / 吸引或招揽某人进入（机构、办公室、诊所等）”。
> B: I haven't been feeling well for a few days.
> A: I'm sorry to hear that. Can you tell me what's wrong?
> B: I have a sore throat and a runny nose. **a sore throat**：名词短语，意为“嗓子疼 / 咽喉肿痛”。**sore** 意为“疼痛的、酸痛的”，_throat_ 意为“喉咙”。**a runny nose**：名词短语，意为“流鼻涕”。**runny** 意为“流鼻水的、流脓的”，_nose_ 意为“鼻子”。
> A: I see. Do you have a cough as well? **as well**：副词短语，意为“也、还”（通常放在句尾，相当于 _too_ 或 _also_）
> B: Yes, a little, especially at night.
> A: How long have you had these symptoms? **these symptoms**：名词短语（复数），意为“这些症状”（单数形式为 _symptom_）
> B: For about four days now.
> A: Have they been getting better or worse?
> B: They're getting a bit worse.
> A: Do you have a fever?
> B: Yes, sometimes in the evening.
> A: Have you been able to sleep well?
> B: Not really. I wake up a lot during the night.
> A: Are you taking any medicine at the moment?  **at the moment**：非常高频的固定时间状语，意为“目前、现在、眼下”（相当于 _now_ 或 _currently_）。
> B: Just some cold medicine from the pharmacy.
> A: Does it help?
> B: Only a little.
> A: Alright. Let me check your throat and listen to your breathing.
> B: Okay.
> A: Please open your mouth and say "ah."
> B: Ah.
> A: Your throat looks red, and your breathing sounds fine.
> B: Is it serious?
> A: No, it's not serious.
> B: It looks like a mild infection. **a mild infection**：名词短语，意为“一次轻微的感染 / 轻度感染”。**mild** 意为“温和的、轻微的、不严重的”。**infection** 意为“感染、传染病”。
> A: That's good to hear.
> B: I'll give you some medicine for the infection and the fever.
> A: How should I take it? 我应该怎么服用它呢？
> B: Take it twice a day, after meals.
> A: Alright.
> B: You should also rest at home and drink plenty of warm fluids. **fluids** 意为“液体、流质食物”（单数形式为 _fluid_）。在医嘱中，医生常用这个词来泛指水、茶、清汤等有助于身体补水的流质。
> A: How many days should I rest?
> B: About three to five days.
> A: Okay, I understand.
> B: If you don't feel better in a week, please come back.
> A: I will. Thank you very much, doctor.
> B: You're welcome. Take care and get well soon.

## 2. On the Airplane
> A: Hello. What can I get for you to drink today? **What can I get for you...**：非常地道且礼貌的服务用语，意为“我能为你拿/准备什么……”（常用于餐厅、飞机等服务场景，相当于 _What would you like..._）。
> B: Hi. Can I have a Pepsi, no ice, please? **a Pepsi**：名词短语，意为“一杯/罐百事可乐”。
> A: I'm sorry, we only have Coke products.
> B: Is a Coke okay?
> A: Sure, that's fine.
> B: Would you like ice with that?
> A: No ice, please.
> B: Alright. And for your meal, We have pasta with chicken or beef with vegetables.
> A: Which one would you like?
> B: I'll take the pasta, please.
> A: Okay. Here you go. **Here you go**：非常地道且高频的日常交际用语，意为“给你 / 请拿好”（通常在递给别人东西时使用）。
> B: Thank you.
> A: Would you like anything else?
> B: Actually, yes. I noticed the seatbelt sign is on. but I really need to use the restroom. Is it okay if I go now?
> A: If you can wait a little, that would be better. The captain should turn off the seatbelt sign in about ten minutes.
> B: Okay, I can wait. One more thing. Is it possible to get another blanket? I'm feeling a bit cold.
> A: Of course. I'll bring one right over.
> B: Thank you. I really appreciate it.
> A: You're welcome. Please enjoy your meal.
> B: Thanks a lot.

## 3. Buying Train Tickets
> A: Good morning. How can I help you?
> B: Hi, I'd like to buy a train ticket.
> A: Sure. Where are you traveling to?
> B: I'm going to Boston.
> A: One-way or round-trip?
> B: One-way, please.
> A: When would you like to travel?
> B: This Friday morning.
> A: Okay. Do you have a preferred departure time? **a preferred departure time**：名词短语，意为“一个偏好的出发时间”。 **preferred** 意为“首选的、偏好的”。**departure time** 意为“出发时间、开车时间”（动词形式为 _depart_，意为“出发/启程”）。 
> B: Around 9 a.m., if possible.
> A: Let me check the schedule for you... We have a train leaving at 8:45 a.m. and another at 9:30 a.m.
> B: The 9:30 train would be great.
> A: Alright! Would you like a standard seat or business class?
> B: A standard seat is fine.
> A: Got it. That will be $45.
> B: How would you like to pay?
> A: I'll pay by card.
> B: No problem. Here's your ticket.
> A: The train will depart from platform 3.
> B: Thank you.
> A: You're welcome. Please arrive at least 15 minutes early.
> B: I will. Thanks for your help.
> A: Have a nice trip.

## 4. How Was Your Trip?

> B: Yes, it was very peaceful.

> A: That's convenient.
> B: Yes, I could walk everywhere.
> A: What did you do during the day?
> B: I went swimming, walk around, and tried local food.
> A: Sounds fun.
> B: It really was.
> A: Did you take any photos?
> B: Yes, I took a lot of photos.
> A: I'd love to see them sometime. 我很乐意找个时间看看它们
> B: Sure, I'll show you later.
> A: How was the food there?
> B: It was delicious, especially the seafood.
> A: I'm getting hungry just hearing about it.
> B: Me too!
> A: Was the trip too short?
> B: A little. I wish I had stayed longer.
> A: Well, it's good to be back too.
> B: Yes, but I already miss it.
> A: Would you go there again?
> B: Definitely. I'd love to go back someday.
> A: That's great. I'm glad you enjoyed your trip.
> B: Thank you. It was a wonderful break.

## 5. Coffee Shop Chat
> A: I'm really glad we could meet today.
> B: Me too. It's been a while since we last talked. 我也是。我们好久没聊天了。**It's been a while...**：现在完成时的固定句型，意为“已经有一段时间了 / 好久没……了”（_a while_ 表示“一会儿、一段时间”）。
> A: Yeah, things have been pretty busy lately. **things have been...**：现在完成时的结构，意为“情况一直…… / 事情一直处于……状态”。_pretty_ 在这里作副词，修饰 busy，表示程度“相当、挺”。**lately**：副词，意为“最近、近来”（常与现在完成时连用）
> B: Same here. Work has been taking up most of my time. 我也是。工作占据了我大部分的时间。- **has been taking up**：现在完成进行时结构，意为“一直占用着 / 一直占据着”（强调从过去开始一直持续到现在的状态）。**take up**：经典动词短语，意为“占据（时间、空间等）”。
> A: I know that feeling.
> B: How do you like your new job so far. **so far**：高频时间短语，意为“到目前为止 / 迄今为止”（通常与现在完成时或一般现在时连用，用来限定某一阶段的情况）。
> A: It's good, but I'm still getting used to it. 挺好的，不过我还在适应中。**get used to...** 是一个非常高频且地道的固定搭配，意为“习惯于…… / 适应……”
> B: The schedule is a bit different.
> A: That makes sense. At least the coffee here is good.
> B: Definitely. I come here almost every week.
> A: Really? I didn't know that.
> B: Yeah, it's kind of my favorite place to relax. 是啊，这算是散心 / 放松最喜欢去的地方了。**it's kind of...**：非常高频的口语表达，意为“有点儿、算是、可以说是一种……”（用来缓和语气，使表达不那么绝对）。
> A: I can see why it's quiet and cozy. **I can see why...**：非常地道的日常句型，意为“我能明白/看出为什么……”（常用于亲身体验后，赞同或理解别人的评价）。**cozy** 意为“舒适的、惬意的、温馨的”。
> B: Exactly. So, what have you been up to lately? 确实如此。那么，你最近都在忙些什么呢？- **What have you been up to...**：非常地道的日常问候句型，意为“你一直在忙什么 / 最近在做什么”（现在完成进行时，用来询问一段时间以来的活动或近况）。**lately**：副词，意为“最近、近来”。
> A: Not much, to be honest. - **Not much**：非常高频的口语简略回答，意为“没多少、没什么特别的”（常用来回答 _What have you been up to?_ 等询问近况的问题）。**to be honest**：极其地道的插入语短语，意为“说实话、老实说”（用来开门见山地表达真情实感或客观情况）。
> B: I've been trying to exercise more and eat better. 我最近一直在尝试多锻炼、吃得更健康。**I've been trying to...**：现在完成进行时结构（_I have been trying to..._），意为“我一直试图/在尝试去……”（强调从过去某一时间开始持续到现在的动作）。
> A: That's great. I'v been thinking about doing the same.
> B: We could keep each other motivated. 我们可以互相鼓励 / 互相监督保持动力。**keep each other motivated**：核心动词短语，意为“让彼此保持动力 / 互相激励”。**motivated**：形容词，意为“有积极性的、受鼓舞的、有动力的”。
> A: I like that idea. Maybe we can meet here once a week.
> B: That sounds perfect. Coffee and a good chat.
> A: You can't go wrong with that. 你这个做法错不了
> B: Agreed. I'm really happy we made time today. 同意。我真的很高兴我们今天能抽出时间聚在一起。**Agreed**：过去分词作感叹语，意为“同意 / 赞同 / 说得对”（是 _I agree_ 的简略说法，在口语中非常常用）。**make time** 是一个非常地道的固定搭配，意为“抽时间、挤出时间（去做某事）”。
> A: Me too. We should do this more often.

## 6. Waiting for the Bus
> A: Hi. Are you waiting for the bus too?
> B: Yes, I am. I just got here.
> A: Do you know which bus goes downtown?
> B: Yes, it's bus 12.
> A: Oh, perfect. That's the bus I need.
> B: It usually comes every 10 minutes.
> A: That's good. The wait isn't too long.
> B: Yes, and today is a nice day.
> A: It really is. The weather feels great.
> B: A good day to be outside. 今天是个出门（呼吸新鲜空气/户外活动）的好日子。**A good day to...**：非常地道的日常句型，意为“一个适合做……的好日子”
> A: Where are you heading today? **head** 在这里作动词，意为“前往、朝……方向去”（非常地道，常用来替代 _Where are you going?_）
> B: I'm going to the train station.
> A: Oh, okay. That's a popular stop.
> B: Yes, a lot of people get off there.
> A: How long have you been waiting?
> B: Just about 5 minutes.
> A: That's not bad at all. **at all**：副词短语，意为“根本、全然”（常用于否定句句末，用来加强否定或肯定语气的程度，在这里表示“一点也不差”）。
> B: No, it's fine.
> A: Do you take this bus often?
> B: Yes, I take it almost every day.
> A: Oh, then you know it well.
> B: Yes, it's usually pretty reliable. - **pretty** 在这里作副词，表示程度“相当、挺”。 **reliable** 意为“可靠的、可信赖的”（在这里用来形容公共交通工具按时发车、让人放心）。
> A: Oh, I think the bus is coming now.
> B: Yes, that's Bus 12.
> A: Great. Let's get ready to get on.
> B: Sure. Let's go.

## 7. Morning Commute
**Commute**：名词，意为“通勤、上下班往返行程”（作动词时意为“上下班往返、通勤”）。
> A: Good morning. You're out early today.
> B: Morning. Yeah, I try to leave early. The roads are quieter.
> A: That's true. Mornings feel calmer before seven.
> B: They do. It help me wake up slowly. 确实如此。它能帮我慢慢清醒过来。
> A: Same here. I don't like rushing in the morning. **rushing**：动词 _rush_ 的动名词形式，意为“匆忙、仓促行事”。
> B: Me neither. I need a bit of quiet time. 我也不喜欢。 我需要一点安静的时间
> A: Did you sleep okay last night?
> B: Pretty well. I actually went to bed earlier than usual.
> A: That always helps. I stayed up a little too late. **stayed up**：动词短语 _stay up_ 的过去式，意为“熬夜、不睡觉”。**a little too late**：修饰短语，意为“稍微有点太晚了”。
> B: Yeah, that makes mornings harder. **makes mornings harder**：使役结构（_make + 宾语 + 形容词比较级_），意为“让早晨变得更难/更难受”。 **harder** 是形容词 _hard_ 的比较级，在这里表示困意更浓、起床更辛苦。
> A: At least the weather is nice today.
> B: It is. Walking feels much better when it's sunny.
> A: Do you usually walk all the way, or take the bus?
> B: I take the bus most days, but I like walking to the stop.
> A: Same. It helps me clear my head.
> B: Exactly. I usually listen to music on the way.
> A: Oh, I do that too. It puts me in a good mood.
> B: Podcasts work for me as well, especially short ones. **work for me**：非常地道的日常口语惯用语，意为“对我挺合适、行得通、合我心意”。**as well**：副词短语，意为“也、同样”（常用于句末，相当于 _too_）。
> A: Yeah, something light is perfect in the morning. 是啊，早上吃点（或喝点）清淡的东西再合适不过了。
> B: Did you manage to eat breakfast? **manage to do sth.** 表示“设法完成某事（通常指克服了某种困难、时间紧迫或不便）”。
> A: Just something simple. Toast(烤面包) and coffee.
> B: That's my usual breakfast too.
> A: I can't really function without coffee. **function**：动词，意为“正常工作、运转、发挥功能”（在口语中常用来形容人在睡眠不足或没喝咖啡时思维迟钝、无精打采、无法正常进入状态）。
> B: Same here. Mornings are impossible without it.
> A: Looks like the bus is coming.
> B: Finally. It's right on time today. **Finally**：副词，意为“终于、总算”（常用于期待已久的事情发生时表达松了一口气的喜悦）
> A: That's a nice start to the day.
> B: It is. Hopefully work goes smoothly too. **Hopefully**：副词，意为“但愿、怀着希望地”（常用于句首或句中表达良好的祝愿或期待）。**work goes smoothly**：主谓结构，意为“工作进展顺利”。

## 9. Turning Down the Music
> A: Hi, sorry to bother you. **sorry to bother you**：非常经典且高频的交际惯用句，意为“抱歉打扰你了 / 不好意思麻烦你一下”。 **bother** 在这里作动词，意为“打扰、麻烦”。
> B: Oh, hi. No problem.
> A: I live in the apartment next door. I just wanted to ask about the music.
> B: Oh, I'm sorry. Is it too loud?
> A: Yes, it's a little loud in my place. The sound comes through the walls.
> B: I see. I just moved in yesterday, so I didn't realize the walls were so thin.
> A: Oh, welcome to the building.
> B: Thank you. I'm still getting used to everything.
> A: That makes sense. I didn't want to complain, I just needed a bit of quiet.
> B: I completely understand. I'll turn the music down right now.
> A: I appreciate that. Thank you for being so kind.
> B: Of course. I don't want to bother my neighbors.
> A: These apartments are nice, but the sound travels easily.
> B: Yeah, I can tell. Is this volume okay now? 是的，我能听得出来（或者：看得出来）。现在这个音量可以了吗？**Yeah, I can tell**：非常地道的日常口语惯用语，意为“是的，我看得出来 / 听得出来（心领神会）”。
> A: Yes, that's much better. Thank you so much.
> B: No problem at all. Please let me know if it's ever too loud.
> A: I will. And welcome again.
> B: Thanks. Have a nice day.
> A: You too.

## 10. Canceling a Date
> A: Hi, it's me. Do you have a minute?
> B: Hi. Yes, of course. What's up?
> A: I'm really sorry, but I don't feel very well today.
> B: Oh no. What's wrong?
> A: I have a headache and feel a bit tired.
> B: That doesn't sound good.
> A: I don't think I can go out tonight. 我想我今晚可能没办法出门了
> B: I understand.
> A: I'm really sorry to cancel our movie plans.
> B: That's okay. Your health comes first. **comes first**：动词短语，意为“处于首位、最重要、优先”（_come first_ 是一种生动形象的表达，表示在权衡轻重时，某事排在第一位）。
> A: Thank you for understanding.
> B: Of course. Are you going to rest at home?
> A: Yes, I think that's best.
> B: Have you taken any medicine?
> A: Just some painkillers. I hope they help. 就吃了一些止痛药。希望它们能管用（能缓解疼痛）。
> B: Make sure you drink some water and rest. **Make sure you...**：非常高频的英语口语交际句型，意为“务必要…… / 确保你一定要……”（常用于叮嘱、提醒对方做某事）。
> A: I will. I feel bad about canceling.
> B: Don't worry about it.
> A: Can we watch the movie another day?
> B: Sure. We can go when you feel better.
> A: That sounds great. Maybe this weekend?
> B: Yes, that works for me. 
> A: Thanks again for being so understanding.
> B: No problem at all. Take care.
> A: I will. Talk to you later.
> B: Get well soon. Bye.
