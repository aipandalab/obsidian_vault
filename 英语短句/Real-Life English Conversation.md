> B: Confusing? It's written on the cover.
> A: I know. But I didn't read it carefully.
> B: Why not?
> A: My English is not good. I read fast and made a mistake.

> A: "Introduction to Psychology." But I bought "Psychology 101.  **Introduction to...**：固定短语，意为“……导论 / ……入门 / 关于……的介绍”（学术教材或课程名称中非常高频的经典搭配）。**Psychology**：名词，意为“心理学”（柜台上和店员手里拿的书名都与这门学科有关）。

> A: Yes! Different books. Different authors
> B: Hmm. That is a mistake.

> B: That's a good lesson.

> B: And "refund"! Don't forget refund.
> A: Yes! Refund is money back.

> A: Care? My suit is ruined! **ruined** 是动词 _ruin_（毁坏、毁灭）的过去分词形式，在这里作表语，意为“被毁坏的、完蛋的”。

> B: The sleeves look fine to me.

> B: Maybe the suit was always short.

> A: Will it work?
> B: I can try. But no promises.
> A: No promises? I need a guarantee! **guarantee** 是名词，意为“保证、保修、担保”。
> B: Guarantee? I can't guarantee it.
