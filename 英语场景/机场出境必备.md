1. Check in, Checking baggage. 办理登机手续，行李托运
```
Where are you flying to today? 你今天旅程的目的地是哪里？

I'm going to ...
eg. I'm going to Sydney. 我要去悉尼。

I'll go to ... first, and transfer to ...
eg. I'll go to Singapore first, and transfer to Sydney. 我先去新加坡，然后转机去悉尼

如果短暂停留(少于24小时的转机)，可以用：transfer、layover、
如果短暂停留超过24小时，可以用：stopover

I'll have a stopover in ... and get a connecting flight to ... 
我将在...中途停留，然后搭乘转机航班飞往...
eg. I'll have a stopover in Singapore, and get a connecting flight to Sydney.
我将在新加坡中途停留，然后搭乘转机航班前往悉尼

connecting flight 转机航班

May I have your passport and ticket? 可以给我你的护照和机票吗？
Sure, here you go. 当然可以，给你。

Are you checking in any baggage? 你有需要托运的行李吗？
Yes, ... suitcase(s). 有的，...个行李箱。
eg. Yes, one suitcase. 有的，一个行李箱。
eg. No, I just have a carry-on. 不，我只有一个随身行李。

carry-on随身行李
hand baggage 手提行李

Please put your suitcase on the scale. 请将你的行李箱放在秤上。
The weight limit is 20kg. 重量限制为20公斤

Your baggage is ...kg overweight. 您的行李超重...公斤。
eg. Your baggage is 2kg overweight. 您的行李超重2公斤。

行李的英文单词：(UK)luggage  (US)baggage

There'll be an extra charge of 💲100 for the overweight picec. 
超重件需加收100美元的额外费用

Pleas mark this bag as "fragile". 请将此行李标记为“易碎”

Do you have any electrical goods in your baggage? 您的行李中有电器产品吗？
Please remove the batteries and carry them in your hand baggage. 

Please place sharp items into checked baggage. 请将尖锐物品放入托运行李中。

Would you like a window seat or an aisle seat? 你想要靠窗还是靠走道的座位呢？
I'd like to have a window seat. 我想要一个靠窗的座位。
I'd like to have an aisle seat. 我想要一个靠走道的座位。

Here's your boarding pass and your passport. 这是您的登机牌和护照。

boarding pass 登机牌

Your seat number is ...
Your seat number is 55B. 你的座位号是55B

Your flight leaves from Gate ...
你的航班从...号登机口起飞。

eg. Your flight leaves from Gate 23. 您的航班从23号登机口起飞。

When is the boarding time? 登机时间是什么时候？

Your flight will begin boarding at ...
eg. Your flight will begin boarding at 10am. 您的航班将于上午10点开始登机。

Please arrive at the gate at least 30 minutes before the departure time.
请在出发时间前至少30分钟到达登机口

Excuse me, where is Gate ...? 打扰一下，请问...号登机口在哪里？
eg. Excuse me, where is Gate 23? 

How long is the layover? 中转时间多长？

You have a ... -hour layover in ... 你在...停留...小时
eg.You have a 4-hour layover in Singapore. 你在新加坡停留4小时。
```
