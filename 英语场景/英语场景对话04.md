## 1. Asking for a Seat Change on a Plane
> A: Excuse me. Can you help me, please.
> B: Of course. What do you need?
> A: I would like to change my seat if possible.
> B: All right. What seems to be the problem?
> A: I am sitting in the middle seat, and I feel uncomfortable.
> B: I see.
> A: I was hoping for a window seat.
> B: Let me check if there is one available.
> A: Thank you very much.
> B: You are welcome.
> A: I don't want to cause trouble.
> B: It is no trouble at all.
> A: Thank you.
> B: What is your seat number now?
> A: It is 18B.
> B: All right. Please wait a moment.
> A: Of course.
> B: I'm checking the seat map now.
> A: Okay.
> B: Yes, I see one empty window seat.
> A: Really? That it great.
> B: It is in row 22.
> A: That is fine.
> B: Good. Then I can move you there.
> A: Thank you so much.
> B: You are welcome.
> A: Do I need to move now?
> B: Please wait until the seat belt sign is off. 请稍等，直到安全带指示灯熄灭为止
> A: All right. I understand.
> B: Then you can take your bag and move safely.
> A: Okay.
> B: Are you traveling alone today?
> A: Yes, I am.
> B: Then the seat change is easy.
> A: Good.
> B: Is this a long flight for you? 你觉得这次航程很漫长吗？
> A: Yes, about four hours.
> B: Then a more comfortable seat will help.
> A: Yes, that is why I asked.
> B: I understand.
> A: I always feel better near the window.
> B: Many people do.
> A: It is quieter for me too.
> B: Yes, I can understand that.
> A: Thank you for being so kind.
> B: Of course. That is my job.
> A: I really appreciate it.
> B: No problem at all.
> A: So I move to row 22 after the sign is off?
> B: Yes, exactly. Seat 22A.
> A: Perfect. I will remember that.
> B: Good.
> A: Thank you again for your help.
> B: You are very welcome.
> A: Have a good flight.
> B: You too. Enjoy your new seat.

## 2. Reporting a Lost Bag at the Airport
> A: Excuse me. I need some help. My bag is missing.
> B: I'm sorry to hear that. Did you lose it  here at the airport?
> A: Yes, I think so.
> B: All right. Can you tell me what happened?
> A: I arrived on a flight this morning. I waited at the baggage area, but my suitcase never came.
> B: I see.
> A: Everyone else took their bags, but mine didn't arrive.
> B: I understand. Let me help you report it.
> A: Thank you very much.
> B: You are welcome.
> A: I'm a little worried because I have all my clothes in that bag.
> B: That is understandable.
> A: And I'm only here for three days.
> B: All right. Let's start with your flight number.
> A: It was Flight 248 from Boston.
> B: Thank you.
> A: Do you need my ticket too?
> B: Yes, please. And your baggage tag if you have it.
> A: I have both. Here they are.
> B: Great. That helps a lot.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Can you describe your suitcase?
> A: Yes, It's a large black suitcase.
> B: All right.
> A: It has a blue ribbon(丝带) on the handle(把手).
> B: That is useful.
> A: And there is a small sticker(贴纸) on the side.
> B: Good. That makes it easier to identify.
> A: I hope so.
> B: Do you know if your name is on the bag?
> A: Yes, there is a name tag with my phone number.
> B: Perfect.
> A: What happens now? 接下来会发生什么？/ 现在该怎么办？
> B: I will enter the information into our system.
> A: Okay.
> B: Then we will check if the bag is still at the airport or on another flight.
> A: I see.
> B: If we find it, we can send it to your hotel.
> A: That would be very helpful.
> B: What's the name of your hotel?
> A: It is the Park Central Hotel.
> B: Thank you.
> A: Do you think you can find it today?
> B: I can't promise, but we will do our best.
> A: I understand.
> B: Here is your report number.
> A: Please keep it safe.
> B: Thank you very much.
> A: You are welcome. We will call you if we have news.
> B: All right. I really hope it turn up(（丢失的物品等）被找到、出现、现身) soon.
> A: I hope so too.

## 3. Talking to a Receptionist(接待员 / 前台人员) at a Clinic
> A: Good morning. I have an appointment(预约) at the clinic.
> B: Good morning. What's your name, please?
> A: My name is Sarah Miller.
> B: Thank you. Let me check.
> A: Of course.
> B: Yes, I found your name. Your appointment is at ten fifteen.
> A: Yes, that is correct.
> B: Good. Please take a seat for a moment.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Is this your first visit here?
> A: Yes, it is.
> B: Then I need you to fill out this form.
> A: Okay.
> B: It asks for your address and phone number.
> A: Okay.
> B: Please also write the reason for your visit.
> A: I understand.
> B: Do you have your ID with you?
> A: Yes, I do. Here it is.
> B: Thank you very much.
> A: You are welcome.
> B: And do you have your health card?
> A: Yes, here it is too.
> B: Perfect.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Are you feeling okay today?
> A: Not really. I have had a sore throat since yesterday.**a sore throat**：名词短语，意为“嗓子痛 / 喉咙发炎”（_sore_ 表示疼痛的、酸痛的，_throat_ 是喉咙）。
> B: I'm sorry to hear that.
> A: And I feel very tired too.
> B: I understand. The doctor will speak with you soon.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Have you been here before with another doctor?
> A: No, this is my first time at this clinic.
> B: All right.
> A: Is the waiting time long today?
> B: Not too long. Maybe fifteen minutes.
> A: That is fine.
> B: Good.
> A: Is there water I can drink while I wait?
> B: Yes, there is a water machine in the corner.
> A: Thank you.
> B: You are welcome.
> A: I finished the form now.
> B: Great. Let me take it.
> A: Here you are.
> B: Thank you. Everything looks fine.
> A: Good.
> B: The doctor will call your name when ready.
> A: All right. I will wait here.
> B: Perfect.
> A: Thank you for your help.
> B: You are welcome. I hope you feel better soon.
> A: Thank you very much.
> B: No problem at all.

## 4. Booking a Table at a Restaurant by Phone
> A: Is this Green Garden Restaurant?
> B: Yes, it is. Good afternoon. How can I help you?
> A: Hello. I would like to book a table, please.
> B: Of course. For what day?
> A: For this Saturday evening.
> B: All right. What time would you like?
> A: Around seven thirty, please.
> B: Let me check.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Yes, we have a table available at seven thirty.
> A: Great.
> B: How many people will be coming?
> A: Four people.
> B: A table for four at seven thirty on Saturday.
> A: Yes, that is right.
> B: May I have your name, please?
> A: Yes. My name is Daniel Brown.
> B: Thank you, Mr. Brown.
> A: You are welcome.
> B: Can I also have your phone number?
> A: Of course. It is 555-3841.
> B: Thank you.
> A: Do you need anything else?
> B: No, that is all for the booking.
> A: Good.
> B: Would you like a table inside or outside?
> A: Inside, please.
> B: Of course.
> A: It may be a little cold in the evening.
> B: Yes, that makes sense. 是的，这讲得通 / 这很有道理 / 我明白了。
> A: Is the restaurant busy on Saturdays?
> B: Yes, usually very busy.
> A: Then I am glad I called early.  那我很庆幸我打电话（来预约）得早。
> B: Yes, that was a good idea.
> A: We are celebrating my sister's birthday.
> B: Oh, that is nice.
> A: Yes, she likes your restaurant very much.
> B: We are happy to hear that.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Would you like to note that it is a birthday dinner?
> A: Yes, please. That would be nice.
> B: All right. I will write that down.
> A: Thank you very much.
> B: You are welcome.
> A: Do you close late on Saturday?
> B: Yes, we close at eleven.
> A: Perfect.
> B: So your table is booked for four people. Saturday at seven thirty, under Daniel Brown.
> A: Yes, exactly.
> B: Great. We look forward to seeing you.
> A: Thank you.
> B: Have a nice day.
> A: You too. Goodbye.
> B: Goodbye.
