## 1. Requesting an Airplane Bassinet for an Infant
为婴儿申请飞机摇篮。_bassinet_ 指专为婴儿准备的“便携式小床 / 婴儿摇篮”。**Infant**：名词，意为“婴儿 / 幼儿”（通常指1岁以下的婴儿）。
> A: Excuse me. I'm traveling with my baby.
> B: Of course. How old is your infant?
> A: She's four months old and quite small.
> B: Then she qualifies for our onboard bassinet.  **qualifies for...**：动词 _qualify_ 的第三人称单数形式，意为“有资格、符合……的条件”。在机场柜台或客服场景中，常用来核对乘客是否满足行李、座位或婴儿设施的申请标准。 **onboard**：形容词或副词，意为“在机上的、船上的、车上的”。
> A: Oh, that's wonderful. Can I request one now?
> B: Yes, but I need to check availability.  非常高频实用的商务及服务业短语，意为“核实/检查是否有空余、是否可供使用
> A: How many bassinets does the plane have?
> B: This aircraft has four bassinets in total. **aircraft**：名词，意为“飞机、航空器”。**in total**：固定短语，意为“总共、合计”，常用于说明某项资源的总体数量或限额。
> A: Are they all booked for this flight?
> B: Let me look. No, two are still free.
> A: Please reserve one for me if possible.
> B: I can, but you need a bulkhead seat. **bulkhead seat**：航空专业术语，意为“舱壁座位 / 隔板排座位”。通常指飞机舱内正对厨房、洗手间或客舱隔板的第一排座位。
> A: What is a bulkhead seat exactly?
> B: It's the front row with extra legroom. **front row**：意为“前排”。在飞机上通常指每个舱段的最前面一排。**extra legroom**：非常实用的航空与出行短语，意为“额外的腿部空间 / 更宽敞的腿部空间”。
> A: Is there an extra charge for that?
> B: For infant bassinets, there's no extra fee.
> A: That's a relief. My budget is tight.  **That's a relief.**：非常高频的日常口语交际句型，意为“太好了 / 真是松了一口气 / 悬着的心终于落地了”。用于得知一件令人担忧的事有了好结果时的即时反应。**budget is tight**：固定搭配，_budget_ 意为“预算”，_tight_ 意为“紧缩的、拮据的”。整句用来表达“手头比较紧 / 预算有限”。
> B: I understand. Traveling with babies is expensive.
> A: When can I actually use the bassinet? **actually**：副词，意为“实际上、到底、究竟”。在此处用于加强语气，表达对具体细节的探究。
> B: Only during cruise, not during takeoff or landing. **cruise**：在航空语境中指飞机进入平稳飞行阶段的“巡航、平飞”。**takeoff or landing**：_takeoff_ 意为“起飞”，_landing_ 意为“降落/着陆”。
> A: So I hold her during takeoff?
> B: Yes, with the infant seatbelt we provide. 可以，（使用时）会配合我们提供的婴儿安全带。
> A: How much weight can the bassinet hold?
> B: It supports infants up to eleven kilograms. - **up to**：意为“最高达、多达”。 **eleven kilograms**意为“11公斤”
> A: Good, she only weights six kilograms now.
> B: Perfect. I'll add it to your booking.
> A: Should I arrive early at the gate?
> B: Yes, please arrive about forty minutes early.
> A: Will the crew set it up for me? **the crew**：意为“机组人员 / 全体船员或乘务组”。
> B: Absolutely. Just ask them after takeoff.
> A: What if both bassinets get taken? **get taken**：意为“被占走、被预订满”
> B: I'll mark yours as confirmed right now. 我马上把您的（摇篮名额）标记为已确认。
> A: Thank you. That makes me feel calmer. **calmer**：形容词 _calm_（平静的、镇定的）的比较级形式，意为“更平静的、更安心的”。
> B: You're welcome. Here's your updated boarding pass.
> A: My seat number is 12A, correct?
> B: Yes, 12A, the bulkhead window seat.
> A: Can my husband sit beside me?
> B: I've place him in 12B, right next to you.
> A: That's perfect. Thank you so much.
> B: Have a smooth flight with your little one. **smooth flight**：固定搭配，意为“平稳的飞行 / 旅途顺利”。


## 2. Wheelchair-Accessible Hotel Rooms
**Wheelchair-Accessible**：复合形容词，意为“无障碍的 / 轮椅可通行的”
> A: Hi, I'd like to book an accessible room. 你好，我想预订一间无障碍客房。**accessible room**：意为“无障碍客房”。专门为行动不便或残障人士设计的酒店房间，通常配有更宽敞的门廊、无门槛浴室及扶手等设施。
> B: Certainly. Is it for wheelchair access?
> A: Yes, my father uses a wheelchair full-time. **full-time**：副词短语，意为“全职地、全部时间地”
> B: We have three accessible rooms available.
> A: Do they have wide doorways for the chair?
> B: Yes, all doorways are ninety centimeters wide. 是的，所有门框都有九十公分宽。
> A: What about the bathroom setup inside? 里面的卫生间布局和设施怎么样？
> B: There'r a roll-in shower with grab bars. （房间里）配有带扶手的无门槛淋浴间。**roll-in shower**：专用无障碍词汇，意为“无门槛淋浴间 / 轮椅可直接推进去的淋浴间” **grab bars**：意为“扶手 / 抓杆”。
> A: Is there a seat in the shower?
> B: Yes, a fold-down bench is installed. **fold-down bench**：意为“折叠长凳 / 折叠座椅”。
> A: That's great. Is the bed a good height? 太好了。床的高度合适吗？
> B: Yes, the bed is set a bit lower.
> A: How much is the room per night?
> B: It's one hundred twenty dollars per night.
> A: Does that include breakfast in the morning?
> B: Yes, breakfast is served from seven to ten.
> A: Is the dining area accessible too?
> B: Yes, it's on the ground floor with ramps. **ramps**：名词 _ramp_ 的复数形式，意为“斜坡 / 无障碍坡道”。
> A: What about getting to de upper floors? **the upper floors**：意为“楼上的楼层 / 高层”。在酒店场景中，通常指除了一楼（ground floor）之外的其他客房楼层。
> B: We have two elevators with braille buttons.
> A: Is there accessible parking near the entrance? **accessible parking**：意为“无障碍停车位 / 残疾人专属停车位”。通常位于距离建筑入口最近、最方便出入的位置，车位尺寸也会比普通车位更宽，以便于轮椅上下车。
> B: Yes, two spots right beside the main door.
> A: How far is the room from the elevator?
> B: It's just ten meters down the hallway.
> A: Perfect. Can I book for three nights?
> B: Of course. May I have your name?
> A: It's Sarah Miller, checking in Friday.
> B: Great. What time will you arrive?
> A: Probably around three in the afternoon.
> B: Check-in starts at two, so that's fine.
> A: Do you need a deposit today?
> B: Yes, a fifty-dollar deposit holds the room.
> A: I'll pay my card right now.
> B: Thank you. Your booking is confirmed.
> A: Can you email me the confirmation?
> B: Yes, I'll send it in a few minutes.
> A: One more thing, is the pool accessible?
> B: Yes, there's a lift chair at the pool. **lift chair**：意为“升降椅 / 电动升降椅”
> A: Wonderful. My father will be happy.
> B: We look forward to your stay, Ms. Miller.


## 3. Fragile Packing Services with movers
**fragile packing**：意为“易碎品打包 / 防碎包装”。_fragile_ 专门用来形容玻璃、陶瓷等容易破碎、需要轻拿轻放的物品。**Movers**：名词 _mover_ 的复数形式，意为“搬家公司 / 搬运工”。
> A: Hello, I'm moving next week and need help.
> B: Sure. Do you have many fragile items?
> A: Yes, lots of glassed, plates, and a mirror.
> B: We offer a special fragile packing service.
> A: That sounds perfect. What does it include?
> B: We use bubble wrap and reinforced boxes. **bubble wrap**：意为“气泡膜 / 气泡垫” **reinforced boxes**：意为“加固纸箱 / 加厚纸盒”。_reinforced_ 是动词 _reinforce_（加固、强化）的过去分词作形容词
> A: Do you pack everything yourselves?
> B: Yes, our team wraps each item carefully.
> A: How much does the fragile service cost?
> B: It's about eighty dollars for one room.
> A: I have a kitchen and living room. **living room**：名词，意为“客厅 / 起居室”
> B: Then it would be around one hundred sixty dollars.
> A: Does that price include the boxes?
> B: Yes, all packing materials are included. 
> A: What about my large wall mirror?
> B: We build a custom wooden crate for that. **custom wooden crate**：意为“定制木箱”
> A: Is there an extra fee for the crate?
> B: Yes, the crate adds thirty dollars.
> A: Are the items insured during the move? **insured**：形容词，意为“受保险保障的 / 有保险的”。动词形式为 _insure_（投保）。
> B: Yes, we insure up to two thousand dollars. **insure up to...**：动词短语，意为“为……投保最高达 / 保险额度最高为……”。_up to_ 表示上限。
> A: That's reassuring. What if something breaks?  **reassuring**：形容词，意为“令人安心的 / 给人安慰的 / 消除疑虑的”。动词形式为 _reassure_（使安心）。
> B: We refund the item's declared value. **refund**：动词/名词，意为“退款 / 返还”。**declared value**：意为“申报价值 / 声明价值”。
> A: How long does the packing take?
> B: Four your home, about three to four hours.
> A: Can you come the day before moving?
> B: Yes, We prefer packing one day early.
> A: What time would your team arrive?
> B: We can start at nine in the morning.
> A: That works well for my schedule. 这很符合我的时间安排。” / “这个时间对我挺合适的。
> B: Great. How many rooms total again? **again**：在句末表示“……来着 / 再确认一下”。
> A: Two main rooms plus the mirror.
> B: So one hundred ninety dollars altogether.
> A: Okay, that fits within my budget.
> B: Shall I book you for next Thursday?
> A: Yes, please. My address is 45 Oak Street.
> B: Got it. I'll send a confirmation text. 
> A: Do I need to prepare anything?
> B: Just clear a path to each room. 只要清理出通往每个房间的通道就行了。
> A: Easy enough. Thank you so much.
> B: You're welcome. We'll take great care. **We'll take great care**：意为“我们会非常小心的 / 我们会妥善照顾/保护的”
