1. Can I ... ? 我可以......吗？
	Can I borrow your pen?
	Can I have a glass of water?
	Can I look at those pictures? 
2. Could you please...? 你能不能......?
	Could you please pass the salt? 你能不能把盐递给我？
	Could you please help me?
	Could you please be quieter? 你能不能安静点？
3. I might... 我可能......
	I might go to the party.
	I might win this game.
	I might find the book interesting.
	I might have a cold.
	I might not be able to make it. 我可能无法到场。
	I might go next time. 我可能下次再去。
4. I'll .. 我将......
	I'll think about it.
	I'll give you a call. 
	I'll see you tomorrow.
5. Would you ... 你愿意...... 吗？
	Would you like to dance?
	Would you please send me the directions?
	Would you rather stay or go? 你愿意留下还是离开？
6. Would you like to ... ? 你想...吗？
	Would you like to grab some lunch together? 你想一起吃午饭吗？
	Would you like to join me for a coffee? 你想一起喝杯咖啡吗？
	Would you like to go for walk in the park?
7. Put it ... 把它放在......
	Put it over there.
	Put it in the garbage.
	Put it on the table.
8. What were you ... ? 你在(做)...... ?
	What were you doing last night?
	What were you listening to? 
	What were you thinking about?
9. Would you mind ... ? 你介意...... ?
	Would you mind getting my phone?
	Would you mind doing me a favor? **do someone a favor / do a favor for someone**：帮某人一个忙。
	Would you mind if I opened a window?
10. Where can I ... ? 我在那里可以......?
	Where can I get a taxi?
	Where can I put this?
	Where can I find it?
11. Have you ever ... ? 你曾经......?
	Have you ever played tennis?
	Have you ever been to Europe?
	Have you ever tried sushi?
12. How long have you ... ? 你...... 有多长时间了？
	How long have you known that?
	How long have you had it?
	How long have you waited?
13. He has been ... 他一直......
	He has been looking for you.
	He has been playing the piano.
	He has been traveling.
14. When didi you...? 你什么时候......?
	When did you do it?
	When did you go?
	When did you email me?
15. What time ...? 什么时间...?
	What time did the game start?
	What time is it?
	What time do they close? 什么时间关门？
16. What is he ...? 他在......什么？
	What is he thinking? 他在想什么？
	What is he talking about?
	What is he doing?
17. We can do ...  我们可以做......
	We can do it this afternoon.
	We can do lunch tomorrow. 我们可以明天一起吃午餐
	We can do lots of things together.
18. How many ... ? 有多少......?
	How many times have you gone?
	How many do you want?
	How many books do you have?
19. I must ... 我必须...
	I must obey the law. 我必须遵守法律
	I must get up early tomorrow.
	I must fill in this form.
20. Are you going ... ? 你要......?
	Are you going to buy the shoes?
	Are you going to have lunch with her?
	Are you going to call your mom?
21. I need to ... 我需要......
	I need to throw out the garbage.
	I need to wash the car.
	I need to go shopping.
22. What if ... ?   如果......呢？
	What if I said yes? 如果我说是呢？
	What if we took a different route to get there? 如果我们采取不同的路线到达那里呢？
	What if you are wrong? 如果你错了呢？
23. What should I ... ? 我应该怎么......?
	What should I do about it? 我应该怎么做呢？
	What should I say to her? 我应该对她说什么？
	What should I tell John? 我应该告诉约翰什么？
24. It makes me ... 这让我......
	It makes me happy to hear that.
	It makes me sad to know people are starving. 知道人们正在挨饿让我很难过
	It makes me proud to be with you. 和你一起让我很自豪。
25. I'll be able to ... 我将能......
	I'll be able to drive there.
	I'll be able to shop here more often.
	I'll be able to walk in the park every day.
26. I want to be able to ... 我希望能够......
	I want to be able to travel more.
	I want to be able to see you.
	I want to be able to do things. 我希望能做一些事情
27. I feel like ... 我觉得......
	I feel like taking a break. 我觉得要休息一下
	I feel like I'm doing the right thing.
	I feel like watching a good movie. 我觉得想看一部好电影
28. I can't believe ... 我不敢相信......
	I can't believe how fast time flies.
	I can't believe I made that silly mistake.
	I can't believe we're actually going on vacation. 我不敢相信我们真的要去度假了
29. It's probably ... 这可能是 ...
	It's probably not a good idea. 
	It's probably going to rain. 可能要下雨了
	It's probably on the living room table. 它可能在客厅的桌子上
30. I prefer ... 我更喜欢...
	I prefer baseball over basketball. 我更喜欢棒球而不是篮球。
	I prefer reading books to watching TV.
	I prefer to travel by train instead of by plane.
31. I'm going to ... 我将要...
	I'm going to do some reading. 我要去读一些书
	I'm going to apply for a new job.
	I'm going to clean my room.
32. Let's go for... 我们去...
	Let's go for a walk in the park.
	Let's go for a hike in the mountain. 我们去爬山吧
	Let's go for a bike ride around the neighborhood. 咱们骑自行车在附近转转吧。
33. Why didn't you ... 你为什么不...
	Why didn't you follow the instructions? 你为什么不听从指示？
	Why didn't you answer my email?
	Why didn't you call me? 
34. Don't be so ... 不要这么...
	Don't be so choosy about food. 不要对食物如此挑剔
	Don't be so disrespectful to your elders. 不要对长辈如此不敬
	Don't be so wasteful. 不要这么浪费
35. I need to ... 我需要...
	I need to do the laundry. 我需要洗衣服
	I need to finish this for tomorrow. 我需要为明天完成这个
	I need to exercise regularly to stay healthy.  我需要定期锻炼以保持健康。
36. I must ... 我必须...
	I must go to work now.
	I must hurry up or I'll be late. 我必须抓紧时间，否则就会迟到
	I must renew my driver's license.
37. What are you ... ? 你(做)什么 ... ?
	What are you going to do about it? 你打算怎么做？
	What are you doing tomorrow?
	What are you having for dinner? 你晚餐吃什么？
38. I'm afraid ... 恐怕...
	I'm afraid that's not possible. 恐怕那是不可能的
	I'm afraid we'll be late. 恐怕我们会迟到
	I'm afraid you're out of luck. 恐怕你不走运了
39. No matter what ... 无论怎样 ...
	I'm going no matter what. 无论怎样我都会去
	No matter what, I will always love you.
	We'll figure this out, no matter what. 无论如何，我们会想办法解决的
40. It's time to ... 是时候...
	- It's time to make dinner.
	- It's time to let go of the past and move on. 是时候放下过去，继续前进了。**let go of**：常用动词短语，意为“放手、释怀、抛开（过去的执念、痛苦等）”。
	- It's time to go to bed and get some rest. 是时候上床睡觉，好好休息一下了
41. That's why ...   这就是为什么...
	- He missed the bus, and that's why he arrived late. 他错过了公交车，这就是他迟到的原因
	- She practiced every day, and that's why she can speak English. 她每天都在练习，这就是为什么她能说英语
	- We carefully planned it, and that's why it went smoothly. 我们精心策划，所以才会顺利进行
42. It's not necessary to ... 这没有必要 ...
	- It's not necessary to please everyone. 没有必要取悦所有人
	- It's not necessary to buy a new phone. 没有必要买一部新手机
	- It's not necessary to get me a gift. **get me**：动词短语，意为“给我买 / 为我准备”。
43. You'd better ... 你最好 ...
	- You'd better leave early if you want to make it on time. 如果你想准时到达，你最好早点离开。**make it on time**：非常地道的固定短语，意为“准时到达 / 及时赶到”
	- You'd better apologize for what you said.
	- You'd better take some notes.
44. As long as ... 只要...
	- We can go, as long as the weather is nice.
	- You can borrow my car, as long as you fill it up with gas afterward. 你可以借我的车，只要你事后加满油
	- You can use it, as long as you're careful with it. 你可以使用它，只要你使用时小心一点。**be careful with...** 是固定搭配，意为“小心对待……、爱惜……”。
